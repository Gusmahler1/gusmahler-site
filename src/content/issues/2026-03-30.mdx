## Federal Regulation & CFTC

### Federal government sues three states to defend prediction markets

On April 2, the CFTC and Department of Justice jointly filed federal preemption lawsuits against Arizona, Connecticut, and Illinois — the first time the federal government has sued states to defend prediction markets' regulatory framework. The complaints sought declaratory judgments that the Commodity Exchange Act grants the CFTC exclusive authority over CFTC-registered platforms, along with permanent injunctions and emergency motions for temporary restraining orders.

Each state had escalated enforcement in the preceding months. Connecticut issued cease-and-desist orders in December 2025 to Kalshi, Robinhood Derivatives, and Crypto.com. Illinois Gaming Board issued cease-and-desist letters in January 2026 to Polymarket and others. Arizona acted most aggressively, filing 20 misdemeanor wagering charges against Kalshi on March 17.

CFTC Chairman Selig: "Arizona's decision to weaponize preempted state criminal law against companies that comply with a comprehensive federal regime sets a dangerous precedent."
